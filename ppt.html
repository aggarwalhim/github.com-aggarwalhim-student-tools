PPT Tools

Make PowerPoint files, turn PDFs into slides, and pull the text out of a PPTX. Everything runs in your browser — files are not uploaded.

Images to PPT

Each image becomes one slide (16:9), fitted without stretching.

Text to PPT

Separate slides with a line containing only ---. The first line of each slide is its title; the other lines become bullet points.

PDF to PPT

Each PDF page becomes one slide as a picture. The text on the slides cannot be edited afterwards.
Up to 60 pages. Big PDFs can take a minute on a phone.

Extract text from a PPTX

Choose a .pptx file and get all the slide text. Old .ppt files must first be saved as .pptx in PowerPoint.

Want PPT → PDF? A free website cannot do this well. Open the file in PowerPoint, Google Slides or the PowerPoint phone app and use Save as / Download as → PDF.
