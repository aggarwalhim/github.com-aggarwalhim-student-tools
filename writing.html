Writing Tools

Find and format citations, compare two texts for similarity, and make chat or email links.

Ad space

Citation Finder

Type a paper title or a DOI. It searches Crossref (a free scholarly database) and builds the citation. Needs internet.

Always check the result against the paper. Capital letters, journal names and page numbers can differ from what your teacher wants.

Citation Maker (website or book)

Similarity Check

This compares two texts you give it. It cannot search the internet or books, so it is not a real plagiarism checker. Use it to see how much of your text matches a source you already have.

Search your sentences online

Each button opens a Google search for that exact sentence, so you can see if it is copied from somewhere.

Link Maker

Make a link that opens WhatsApp, Telegram, email, SMS or a phone call with your message ready. Share it anywhere.

A link shortener (like bit.ly) needs a server, so it is not possible on a free static site.
