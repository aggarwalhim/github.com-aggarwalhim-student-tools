Privacy Policy

Applies to the Free Student Tools website and the I Love Tools Telegram bot. Last updated: 31 October 2026.

The short version

The tools run inside your own browser. Your photos, PDFs, documents and typed text are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our server. We do not have accounts, logins or a database, and we do not collect your name, email or phone number.

The Telegram bot

The bot has no server of its own. Its menu button simply opens this website inside Telegram. We do not receive your Telegram name, ID, messages or files. Telegram itself handles your account under its own privacy policy.

What is saved on your device

Some tools (notes, flashcards, saved resume details, exam dates) store what you type in your browser's local storage, on your own device only. It never reaches us. Clearing your browser data erases it, so keep a copy of anything important.

Third-party services the pages use

  • Code libraries from CDNs (cdnjs, jsDelivr) are downloaded when a page loads, so those hosts can see your IP address and browser type in the normal way. Text recognition (OCR) also downloads its language data from a CDN.
  • Google Fonts may be used for the page font.
  • Citation Finder sends the title or DOI you type to the public Crossref service to look up details.
  • Ask AI (if you use it) runs through Puter. Your question is sent to that service under its own terms, and you sign in with your own Puter account.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Ads

We may show ads in the future. Ad networks (such as Google AdSense) can use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure them. If ads are added, this page will be updated and a cookie notice shown where the law requires it.

Children

The tools are meant for students and general use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Questions

Open an issue at the project's GitHub page and we will reply there.
